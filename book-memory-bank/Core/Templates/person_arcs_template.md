## Integration Notes for the AI
*When outlining or drafting a chapter, refer to this matrix to keep a person's emotional portrayal consistent with their real progression point. Do not have someone "learn a lesson" earlier than the real timeline supports, and do not silently drop or contradict a real shift established in an earlier chapter. If it's genuinely unknown how someone felt at a given point, say so rather than inventing certainty.*
