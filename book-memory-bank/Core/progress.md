## What Works

**Strengths**: The name/nickname device ("Shambu," tied to a Shravan-Monday birth and Shiva devotion) gives Chapter 1 a concrete, specific hook that carries both plot and theme without the narrator having to explain anything. On 2026-10-07 the author retitled Chapters 2 and 3 to "The Father" and "The Wife," so all three titles are now short and person-based: a nickname, then two family roles.

**Effective Techniques**: Weaving biographical facts (dates, degrees, jobs) into scene and reflection rather than listing them; explicitly naming factual gaps in prose (e.g., how he financed his education) instead of inventing detail to fill them. Chapter 2 confirmed a working method for scripture: verify every verse against multiple independent web sources before drafting rather than relying on model memory, log results in `Research/scripture_reference_tracker.md`, and let each verse's own irony do most of the moral commentary so the narrator doesn't have to editorialize.

**Flow Areas**: The North America ambition section (Hong Kong trip, IELTS, PR applications) came together easily — it's the most event-driven material available so far. Chapter 2's incident-dense material (the abuse catalogue, the death, the water-glass scene) also came together readily once treated as a composite/representative account rather than a demand to recount all thirty-plus incidents individually.

The 2026-09-28 revision added a fifth Ch 3 verse (Mahabharata, Anushasana Parva 46.5), quoted in its Critical Edition wording after checking the BORI e-text directly. The famous "gods rejoice" line turned out not to be in that edition. See `Research/scripture_reference_tracker.md`.
