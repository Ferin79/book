## Notes

- All Chapter 2 verses (4) and Chapter 3 verses (4) were checked against external sources during drafting (2026-08-20 and 2026-08-27 respectively) rather than drawn purely from model memory, per `CONTEXT.md`'s "Accuracy is the book's armor" rule.
- The Ramayana verse's Devanagari (Ch 2) was reconstructed from a confirmed romanized transliteration rather than fetched directly in Devanagari — worth a second look before publication, though the wording is standard and unambiguous.
- **No verse has been reused across chapters.** Chapter 1 deliberately held all scripture back. Chapter 2 uses Chanakya Niti 2.5, Bhagavad Gita 2.62–63, Mahabharata Vana Parva (Yaksha Prashna), Valmiki Ramayana Ayodhya Kanda 24.1. Chapter 3 uses Bhagavad Gita 16.21, Rigveda 10.85.46, Mahabharata Udyoga Parva 93.48, Bhagavad Gita 3.6 — the Gita is now drawn on twice in one chapter (16.21 and 3.6), doing distinct work (the vices named; the word for hypocrisy), which is acceptable given the chapter's themes map directly onto both.
- **Source spread so far**: Bhagavad Gita ×3 (2.62–63, 16.21, 3.6), Mahabharata ×2 (Vana Parva, Udyoga Parva), Chanakya Niti ×1, Valmiki Ramayana ×1, Rigveda ×1. The Vedas beyond the Rigveda, and the Arthashastra, are still unused — candidates for later chapters.
- **Open pre-publication check**: the Mahabharata Udyoga Parva 93.48 citation's chapter/verse numbering follows one structured Sanskrit source and may vary by recension (Gita Press vs. Critical Edition) — verify against a print edition before finalising, and consider citing by parva + episode only (as done for the Yaksha Prashna verse) if the number proves edition-dependent.
